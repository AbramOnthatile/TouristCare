You’re offline

TouristCare needs an internet connection to send reports, share your location, or contact support. These steps may help while you reconnect:

  • Move to a public, well-lit place and ask a trusted person nearby for help.
  • If you are in immediate danger in South Africa, call 112 from a mobile phone.
  • For urgent medical needs, contact local emergency services directly.
Call 112

TouristCare does not automatically dispatch emergency responders. Offline reports and location sharing are not sent.
